Swiss Marketplace Group · Real estate and Ricardo

AI features in live marketplaces

At Swiss Marketplace Group I worked on AI features for real people listing real things, in the real estate vertical and on Ricardo. 90% of users who generated a listing description kept the text in their final ad.

Description generation for real estate listings

I integrated a description generator into the real estate listing flow. It writes the ad text from what the user selected and the photos they uploaded.

Result: 90% adoption. Of the users who generated a description, 90% used that text in their final ad.

Picture to Listing on Ricardo

On Ricardo, I helped implement Picture to Listing. A user takes a picture of an object, and AI creates the listing with its description and text.

Winning the real estate hackathon

I won the real estate hackathon with a robot that scans a flat and creates a listing with no human in the loop. A hackathon is a good place to test that extreme. In client work, I usually start with AI that people stay in control of.
