Questions about working together

How can you help a business implement AI?

I help businesses identify useful AI opportunities, develop an implementation strategy, choose suitable tools and connect AI to their products and workflows. My focus combines business goals, user needs, product development and design.

Can you develop a complete product from scratch?

Yes. I work across research, product strategy, UX and interface design, design systems, and implementation. The Social Hub and BloodLog are live, self-initiated examples of products I developed with AI assistance.

What experience do you bring to product and design leadership?

I have more than 20 years of experience in product, design and technology. My portfolio includes design and product work for Immoscout24, Rabobank, Deutsche Telekom, BD Rowa and Rakuten Kobo. My resume describes my leadership roles and responsibilities.
