Questions

What does an AI Business Specialist do?

An AI Business Specialist identifies where AI creates value for a company, plans how to introduce it and supports the projects. The role sits between management, business teams and technology.

Is AI Business Specialist a protected title in Switzerland?

Yes. The protected German title is AI Business Specialist mit eidgenössischem Fachausweis, for people who have passed the federal exam. The Swiss education authority SERI (SBFI) approved the exam regulations on 7 October 2025, and the first exam was scheduled for autumn 2026.

Does Louis Henrich hold the federal diploma?

Not yet. He is completing the program at IFA in Zurich and is due to finish in 2027. He already works on AI strategy and AI products, based on over 20 years in product design and leadership.

Is an AI Business Specialist the same as a data scientist?

No. A data scientist mainly builds and analyses models. An AI Business Specialist focuses on the business case, planning and adoption, and works with technical specialists.

When does a company need this kind of support?

Usually when leadership wants to use AI but has no clear priorities, when pilots are not reaching production, or when teams are unsure how their work should change.

Sources on the qualification: Netzwoche and berufsberatung.ch.
