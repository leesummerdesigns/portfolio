What product leadership adds to AI work

Start from the customer

An AI feature has to solve a problem someone already has. I begin with users, the business model and the numbers, and only then look at models and tools.

Ship it and measure it

A demo proves little. I take use cases into live products and judge them by adoption and revenue, like the listing description generator at Swiss Marketplace Group.

Lead the people through it

AI changes how teams work, and that takes leadership as well as tools. I have hired, coached and led designers, and I now coach leaders through the same change.

Build it yourself if needed

I build AI systems myself, so my advice comes from practice. Cortex, The Social Hub and BloodLog are live and open to inspection.

Questions

What is an AI expert with product leadership experience?

Someone who understands what AI can do and also knows how to turn it into a product that customers use and a business can measure. Louis Henrich combines AI strategy and hands-on AI building with over 20 years in product design and leadership.

Why hire an AI expert with a product background?

Because the hard part of many AI projects comes after the technology works: picking the right problem, designing how people use it, getting teams on board and measuring the result. Those are product leadership skills.

Do you only work with product and design teams?

No. I work with leadership teams, product teams and founders. My services cover AI strategy, AI product features, team enablement and agentic workflow pilots.

Do you build AI systems yourself?
