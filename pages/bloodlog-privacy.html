Data Storage

BloodLog is designed to store user data locally on the user's device.

Your blood test history and profile are stored on Your device. The Company does not keep a copy of them on its servers. Some features send Health Data off Your device for processing, as described under "AI Analysis" below.

Because the data is stored locally, You are responsible for managing backups and protecting access to Your device. If the Application is removed from Your device, the locally stored data may also be removed.

Types of Data Collected

Health Data

The Application allows You to record and store health-related information such as blood measurements, notes, logs, and timestamps.

This information may be considered sensitive personal data under applicable privacy laws. BloodLog uses this information solely to provide the core functionality of the Application.

The Company does not store Your Health Data on its servers. When You use AI Analysis, the Health Data needed for the request passes through the Company's server to Anthropic, as described below.

Health data is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

AI Analysis

When You add a blood test from a photo, a PDF or text You enter, the Application sends it to the Company's server for analysis, together with the test date if You entered one and the sex set in Your profile. Photos are converted to a smaller JPEG image first. A document or photo may contain anything printed on it, such as Your name or date of birth.

When You compare blood tests, the Application sends the results and dates of the tests You selected.

The Company's server, hosted by Vercel Inc., passes this data to Anthropic, PBC, which uses its Claude AI models to read Your blood values and write a summary, recommendations or a comparison. The result is returned to the Application and stored on Your device. Vercel and Anthropic act as Service Providers and may process the data in the United States. Where data protection law requires it, these transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

The Company's server handles each request only for as long as it takes to return the result. It does not save the content of Your request and does not write it to its logs. Anthropic may keep the data for a limited period, as described in its privacy policy, and does not use it to train its AI models.

Before the Application first sends a blood test for analysis, it explains what is sent and asks for Your consent. If You do not want Your data processed this way, do not use the features that analyse or compare blood tests.

Usage Data

BloodLog does not collect analytics related to Your health logs or personal entries.

However, limited technical information may be collected by platform providers (such as Apple App Store or Google Play Store) when You download, install, or purchase the Application. This information is processed according to the privacy policies of those platform providers.

When the Application contacts the Company's server, the server and its hosting provider receive technical information needed to deliver the request, such as Your IP address and the time of the request. The server uses Your IP address briefly, in memory, to limit excessive requests. Vercel may keep technical request logs, which do not include the content of Your request, for a short period.

Camera Access

If You choose to use features that require camera access, the Application may request permission to access Your device's camera or photo library.

This access is used solely to let You add blood test results. A photo You submit is sent for analysis as described under "AI Analysis" and is not stored by the Company.

You can enable or disable camera access at any time through Your device settings.

Use of Personal Data

The Company may use limited Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service — To enable core application functionality.
  • To analyse Your blood tests — To read results from the documents, photos or text You submit and to generate summaries and comparisons, with the help of Anthropic as a Service Provider.
  • To manage Your account — To provide access to features within the Application.
  • To manage subscriptions — To verify subscription status and enable premium features.
  • To respond to support requests — To respond to questions or technical support inquiries submitted by users.

BloodLog does not sell, rent, or trade Personal Data.

Subscriptions and Payments

The Application may offer paid subscription plans, including monthly and yearly subscriptions.

Payments are processed by third-party platform providers such as Apple App Store or Google Play Store depending on the device used.

The Company does not store credit card numbers or payment information.

The Application may receive limited information from the platform provider, such as confirmation of payment and subscription status, in order to manage access to premium features.

To apply usage limits, the Application sends the Company's server a signed proof of Your App Store subscription. The server may count Your monthly analyses against Your subscription's transaction ID, using a database provider (Upstash), and keeps each count for no more than 40 days. If You do not have a subscription, the Application instead sends an Apple DeviceCheck token, and the Company may ask Apple to record for that device that its free analysis has been used. These records contain no Health Data.

Payment processing is governed by the privacy policies of the respective platform providers.
