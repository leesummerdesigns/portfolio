AI Expert, AI Business Consultant, Coffee Enthusiast, and Mentor, based in Zurich.

I'm Louis, I grew up in Alicante, Spain where I did my international baccalaureate (IGCSE). Shortly after, I moved to Barcelona to study communication design and media psychology at Elisava University.

After graduating, I moved to Germany where I started working at different agencies. While I first started as a digital art director, I moved fully into user experience in 2013. In the years to come, I deep-dived into all aspects of UX, continuously learning and experimenting with a wide range of methods, tools, and technologies.

In 2017, I worked closely with universities in Barcelona and Amsterdam to develop a fast-learning program for graduates aiming to become product designers. In 2019, I launched the UX Academy with the agency Cocomore in Frankfurt to onboard students into their first real-world work experience.

Over the last years, my work has moved from designing products to helping companies use applied AI. Today I work as an AI expert and AI business consultant in Zurich, Switzerland, and I'm completing my AI Business Specialist diploma at Hochschule IFA Switzerland (2027). At Swiss Marketplace Group I brought AI features into live marketplaces, including a listing description generator where 90% of users who generated text kept it in their final ad. You can read about it in the SMG AI case study.

I help leadership teams shape AI business strategy: analysing the business, assessing opportunities and tools, and turning emerging technology into a realistic roadmap rather than a wishlist. My earlier product and design work includes Immoscout24, Rabobank, Deutsche Telekom, BD Rowa, and Rakuten Kobo, and it's still how I judge whether an AI feature is any good.

I work in native English and German, meeting clients in person throughout Switzerland and collaborating remotely worldwide.

I also work at the organizational level, helping teams adopt AI in a pragmatic and responsible way. This includes redefining workflows, training people on their real work, and identifying where AI creates real leverage versus where it adds noise.

My focus is always on augmentation, not automation for its own sake: freeing teams from repetitive work so they can focus on strategy, creativity, and human insight.

In a nutshell: I bring a multidisciplinary skill set that connects business, product, design and technology with a strong understanding of human nature and behavior. I am a critical thinker with an open mind, a communicator, a facilitator, and someone who knows how to turn emerging technologies like AI into concrete, usable value for teams and organizations.

AI implementation you can inspect

My open-source project Cortex connects a Markdown knowledge base to AI assistants through MCP, with semantic and keyword retrieval and a review inbox for new memories. The project page links to its public macOS release, source code and implementation tests.
