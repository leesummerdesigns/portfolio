What I do as your AI consultant

AI strategy and roadmap

I analyse your business and customers, find the use cases worth funding and turn them into a phased roadmap. How the AI strategy work runs.

AI product features

I design and ship AI features into live products. At Swiss Marketplace Group, 90% of users who generated a listing description kept the text in their final ad.

Team enablement

I train teams on their real work, so new habits stay after I leave. For leaders, I also offer 1:1 AI leadership coaching.

Agentic workflow pilot

I build a working AI automation for one real internal process, so you can see what it does, what it costs and where people stay in the loop.
